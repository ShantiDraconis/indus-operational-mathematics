## How to Use These Resources

1. **For Formal Verification**: Start with Lean 4 tutorial, then adapt to your domain
2. **For Statistics**: Begin with information theory basics, then PMI and related measures
3. **For Implementation**: Use NumPy/SciPy for Python, Eigen for C++
4. **For Validation**: Compare results across multiple implementations
5. **For Publication**: Follow methodology in related archaeological statistics papers

Remember: Ground all claims in empirical evidence and reproducible methods.
